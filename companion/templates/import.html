CSV Import

Supports IMDB list exports (Const column) and Letterboxd exports (Letterboxd URI column with IMDB ID extraction).

How to export from IMDB

  1. Go to your IMDB list or watchlist
  2. Click the three-dot menu → Export
  3. Upload the downloaded CSV here

How to export from Letterboxd

  1. Go to Settings → Import & Export
  2. Click Export Your Data
  3. Upload watchlist.csv or ratings.csv
